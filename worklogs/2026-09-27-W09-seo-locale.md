## Problem found
`SeoHead` forced `og:locale=pt_BR` on every route. That is acceptable as the current fallback, but not as a structural invariant for a global product.

## Implementation
- Added optional `locale` support to `SeoHead`.
- Derives locale from `document.documentElement.lang` when route does not explicitly provide one.
- Normalizes BCP 47 form for `<html lang>` and converts it to Open Graph underscore form.
- Preserves `pt-BR` only as a backward-compatible fallback.
- No public hero/layout/shared visual component was changed.
